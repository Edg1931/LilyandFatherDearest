How to Play

Move — drag the joystick (touch) or use WASD/↑↓←→.

Talk to NPCs — walk up to 📮 🌳 🥐 🪴 🌉 and they'll offer a quest.

Quest markers glow gold. Walk over them or stand close and tap 🐾 interact.

Bond up — actions on the right ring (groom, feed, play, pet) raise your active dog's bond. Bond also rises passively while they follow you.

Tier up — when bond crosses a threshold, the Tier Roll ✨ button appears. Each roll has a chance based on bond + grooming streak. Failed rolls don't reset bond.

House & Shelter — top-right buttons. Buy the Vet (50,000 coins) to start a shelter; pre-seeded strays demonstrate the intake flow.

Progress saves to your browser. Clear it from the help panel.
