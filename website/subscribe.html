How it works

Roast Tuesday. Ship Wednesday. Sip Friday.

Most online coffee shops ship beans days or weeks past roast. We roast in tiny lots and ship within 24 hours, so the bag in your hand is at peak flavor.

  • 1. Pick your plan

    Solo, Pair, or Office. Pause or change any time from your account.

  • 2. Tell us your taste

    Roast level, brew method, and origins to skip — we'll do the rest.

  • 3. We roast Tuesday

    Tiny lots, by hand. We pull the timer when our cupping team approves the cup.

  • 4. You sip Friday

    Ground at request, or whole bean. Free shipping on every order.
