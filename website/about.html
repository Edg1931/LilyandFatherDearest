Our story

From a love of coffee to a love of community.

Avon Lake's small-batch roastery and boba café — where every drink is made by hand and every bean has a backstory.

Love Coffee Roasters started with a simple idea: the best mornings begin with a cup made by someone who actually cares.

What began as a love affair with espresso turned into a roastery. We hunted for green coffee, learned curves, blew up batches, drank tons of cups we didn't love, and slowly figured out the ones we did. Then we opened the doors so we could share them.

Now you'll find us in Avon Lake — pulling shots, shaking boba, and putting tiramisu cream on top of your latte because life is short and dessert in a cup is a perfectly reasonable choice.

Sustainability

Every cup has a footprint. We're shrinking ours.

Coffee is a long supply chain — green beans cross oceans, milk crosses counties, cups end up in landfills. We can't fix everything overnight, but we publish what we're doing.

  • Compostable cupware

    Cups, lids, sleeves, and straws are 100% compostable or fiber-based.

  • Bring-your-own discount

    $0.50 off any drink when you bring a reusable cup. Always.

  • Direct trade

    We pay above commodity for every green bean, traceable to the farm.

  • Spent grounds

    Free for local gardeners. Pick a bag up by the door.
