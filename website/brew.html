The methods

Four ways to a great cup.

Pour Over (V60)

Bright, clean, articulate. Best for single-origin lights and naturals.

  • 1:16Ratio
  • 3:30Total time
  • 200°FWater
  • Med-fineGrind

French Press

Full-bodied, syrupy, easy. The forgiving choice for sleepy mornings.

  • 1:15Ratio
  • 4:00Steep
  • 200°FWater
  • CoarseGrind

AeroPress

Fast, smooth, low-acid. Great for travel; great over ice.

  • 1:14Ratio
  • 1:30Brew
  • 185°FWater
  • FineGrind

Espresso

Concentrated, intense, fast. The base for every milk drink we serve.

  • 1:2Ratio
  • 0:28Pull
  • 9 barPressure
  • Very fineGrind

Cold Brew

Steeped overnight in the fridge — smooth, mellow, never bitter.

  • 1:8Ratio
  • 18 hrSteep
  • 40°FWater
  • CoarseGrind

Chemex

Crisp and clear like a pour-over, with a slower, mellower body.

  • 1:17Ratio
  • 4:30Total time
  • 200°FWater
  • MediumGrind
